================================================================================
  Stonemachia Cheat Mod
================================================================================

An in-game cheat menu for Stonemachia. Press F6 to open toggles for queen form,
god mode, auto-parry, level, jumps, mana, and Topini rat minions.

Requires UE4SS.
Also requires the shared ModMenu runtime (included in the download).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  INSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Extract the zip into your UE4SS Mods folder so you have:

  <Stonemachia install>\Stonemachia\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\StoneMachiaCheatMod\
  <Stonemachia install>\Stonemachia\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\shared\ModMenu\

Both folders are required. The cheat mod includes an enabled.txt file so it
activates automatically — no edits to mods.txt required.

Launch the game. Press F6 to open the cheat menu.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  USAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  F6
      Toggle the cheat menu (dock left/right with the Dock button).

No console commands. Everything is controlled from the menu.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  MENU OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Jumps
      Enable extra jumps. Use Jump Count to pick how many (2–999).
      Only applies while Jumps is ON.

  God Player
      Take no damage in pawn form (DamageMultiplier = 0). Toggle off to restore
      your previous multipliers. Does not protect queen form — use Parry on hit
      with Queen for that.

  Parry on hit
      Auto-parry every incoming hit (full parry rewards). Works in pawn or
      queen form. Persists through death and checkpoints.

  Queen
      Transform into the Queen chess piece (vulnerable, dies in 1–2 hits).
      Toggle off to return to pawn. Persists through death and checkpoints.

  More Mana
      Set mana / max mana to 9999. Toggle off to restore previous values.
      Persists through death while left ON.

  Spawn Topini
      Spawn a Topini rat minion. Click again for more.

  Player Level
      Set level via preset (Default, 10–100, 999, 9999, MAX, etc.).
      Scales health, mana, and piece damage through the game's own stats.
      Persists through death within the session; resets on full game restart.

  Jump Count
      Dropdown for jump amount. Only takes effect when Jumps is enabled.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  USEFUL COMBOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Queen + Parry on hit
      Queen moveset, auto-parries every hit, effectively immortal via parry.

  God Player + Parry on hit
      Invincible pawn with parry rewards on every hit.

  Player Level 9999 + Queen
      Power-fantasy queen that can still die if you leave Parry off.

  Jumps + Jump Count 16 + Queen
      Airborne queen chaos.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Active toggles re-apply after death (~3 s). Queen and Parry on hit also
    re-apply after leaving a checkpoint rest area (~3 s).

  - God Player only affects the pawn damage pipeline. In queen form, use
    Parry on hit for invulnerability-via-parry.

  - Player Level at 9999 can push health toward ~88k and pawn damage toward
    ~7k via the game's own scaling.

  - All modes last for the current play session. Restart the game (or reload
    mods) to clear everything.
